How to track freelance invoices without accounting software

Most freelancers lose money not because clients refuse to pay, but because nobody notices a late invoice until it is 90 days old.

Four things that actually work

  • Write the invoice the day you send it, not at the end of the month.
  • Give every invoice a due date at creation time.
  • Make the overdue ones visually obvious, otherwise you stop looking at the list.
  • Set money aside the moment it arrives, not at tax time.

A spreadsheet with three rules covers this: a due date calculated from the issue date, a status that turns red on its own, and a summary that shows the tax figure you should not touch.
