Debt Snowball vs. Debt Avalanche: Which Is Better?

When you are dealing with multiple debts—such as credit cards, personal loans, car loans, and medical bills—figuring out where to put your extra money can feel overwhelming. Two primary methods dominate personal finance: the Debt Snowball and the Debt Avalanche.

How the Debt Snowball Works

Popularized by Dave Ramsey, the Debt Snowball focuses on psychological momentum. You list your debts from the smallest balance to the largest balance, regardless of interest rates. You pay the minimums on all debts, and throw every extra dollar you have at the smallest debt. Once that debt is wiped out, you take its entire payment plus its minimum and roll it into the next smallest debt. This creates quick wins that keep you motivated.

How the Debt Avalanche Works

The Debt Avalanche focuses strictly on mathematics. You list your debts from the highest interest rate to the lowest interest rate. You pay minimums on all debts and direct all extra cash toward the debt with the highest APR. Financially, this is the most optimal method because it minimizes the total amount of interest paid over time, getting you out of debt faster and cheaper.
