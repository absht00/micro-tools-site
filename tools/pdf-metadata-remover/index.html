Why Strip PDF Metadata Before Sharing Files Online?

When you create a PDF document from Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Adobe Acrobat, or scanned invoices, the software automatically embeds hidden information known as **Document Metadata**. This includes the original author's name, computer username, software version used for creation, editing timestamps, and sometimes previous file paths.

For freelancers, remote workers, and businesses, leaking internal username structures or client names can pose privacy and security risks. Using our Free PDF Metadata Remover ensures your documents are completely sanitized before sending them to clients, uploading them to job portals, or publishing them publicly.

How It Works in Your Browser

Unlike online converters that upload your confidential contracts and invoices to remote cloud servers, our tool runs entirely inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. When you drop a file into the window, the script parses the PDF structure locally, clears out sensitive information fields, and generates a fresh, sanitized PDF for instant download. Your files never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove metadata from password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before metadata removal. Please remove the password protection first.

Does clearing metadata affect the text or layout of my PDF?

No. Metadata cleaning only removes the hidden document properties header. Your text, images, pages, and formatting remain 100% intact.
