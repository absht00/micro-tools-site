Why a meal plan reduces grocery spending more than coupons

Coupons save a percentage on what you were going to buy anyway. A plan changes what you buy.

Four things that actually work

  • Decide the meals once, then let the list derive from the meals.
  • Group the shopping list by supermarket department so you walk the store once.
  • Put a cost per day next to every day, so the expensive ones are visible.
  • Buy for the plan, not for the possibility.

The saving comes from the list following the plan instead of the plan following whatever looks good in the aisle.
