How much do your subscriptions actually cost per year

Almost nobody can answer this question about their own money, and that is exactly why subscription businesses grow.

Four things that actually work

  • Convert every plan to a yearly figure, including the monthly ones.
  • Write down the last time you actually used each service.
  • Sort by cost per year, not by cost per month.
  • Cancel the two most expensive ones you have not opened in 30 days.

The uncomfortable part is the yearly column. A 9.99 monthly charge reads as small and is actually 119.88 a year.
