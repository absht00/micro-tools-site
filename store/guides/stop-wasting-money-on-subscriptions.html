How to Find and Cancel the Subscriptions You Forgot About

The average household pays for more recurring services than it can name from memory. The list is always longer than the guess.

Start from the statements, not from memory. Open the last three months of bank and card statements and write down every charge that repeats. Streaming, storage, apps, newsletters, gym, software, hosting.

For each one, write the real annual cost. A 4.99 euro monthly plan is 60 euro a year; a 99 euro annual plan is 99. Normalising everything to twelve months is what makes the list usable, because monthly prices hide the yearly weight.

Then add one column: the date you last actually used it. Anything untouched for more than sixty days is a candidate for cancellation, regardless of how useful it felt when you signed up.

Cancel in the order of cost divided by use. Not the smallest first: the plan you never open costing 120 euro a year is worth more of your attention than three 2 euro ones.
